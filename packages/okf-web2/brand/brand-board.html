OK, Fine Memory

OK, fine, I’ll remember it for you.

A notebook that remembers things for you and your agents. Plain markdown files, a snail-paced attitude, a perfect memory.

Mascot: Okie, the reluctant snail

Why a snail

Slow and a little lazy on the outside, but nothing that goes into the shell is ever lost. The shell's whorl is drawn like a notebook's spiral binding.

  • Half-lidded eyes on wobbly stalks: unimpressed, never angry
  • Yellow crayon shell (or the workspace's accent)
  • A sigh puff, a pencil, a check-mark trail

Do / Don’t

  • Sigh a little, then help
  • Say what went wrong in plain words
  • Keep outlines wobbly and fills scribbled
  • Write the name as OK, Fine Memory
  • Mock the user or hide errors behind jokes
  • Gradients, shadows, 3D, polished clip-art
  • Crayon-coloured text
  • Angry, sad or winking Okie; a snail without a shell
